What to write about

A price or offer here is wrong. Tell us the page and what it should say, ideally with the provider's own URL. The data is read from the provider's public page, so if that page has changed, the fix belongs in our reading of it.

A provider is missing, or should be removed. If a host runs its own service and publishes a public pricing or promotions page we can read, it qualifies — send the URL. If you run a provider and would rather not be listed, say so and the page comes down.

A link is broken or points somewhere unexpected. We would rather know than not.

Something on the site is inaccurate about you or your business. That takes priority over everything else on this list.

What we cannot help with

Hosting support, billing, refunds, cancellations, renewals, and anything wrong with a server you have bought are the provider's business — not ours. We do not sell hosting, we do not take orders, and we have no access to any provider's systems or to your account with them.

If you write to us about a server problem, the honest answer will be to send you to the provider's own support channel, so you may as well start there.

There is no contact form

Deliberately. A form would mean collecting your name, your address, and whatever you typed into a database belonging to a site that otherwise collects nothing at all. Email keeps the conversation in your own mailbox, under your own control, where you can keep a copy of it.

For the same reason there is no live chat and no ticket system — and no phone number, which would require a company we do not have.

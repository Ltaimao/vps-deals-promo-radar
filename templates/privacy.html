Privacy policy

Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}.

This page describes what this site actually does — not what a privacy policy is supposed to say. Every statement below is either something you can check yourself in your browser's developer tools, or something we state plainly because we do not do it.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. This site has no accounts, no login, no comment section, and no contact form. The one thing you can give it is your email address — only if you use the price-drop alerts form in the footer.

If you subscribe, this is exactly what happens:

  • Your address is stored in a Cloudflare KV store (on Cloudflare's infrastructure, in the same account that hosts this site), together with a random confirmation token and timestamps. Nothing else about you is stored — no name, no IP address, no browsing history.
  • You receive one confirmation email (double opt-in). Until you click the link in it, nothing else is ever sent to you, and the pending record expires after 30 days.
  • Emails are sent from alerts@vpsdealswire.com and delivered through Resend (our email delivery provider). Replies go to contact@vpsdealswire.com.
  • After you confirm, you get an email only when a tracked deal drops to a new lowest price. No newsletters, no digests, no marketing — the subscription does one thing and the form says what it is.
  • Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Clicking it deletes all records for your address immediately — no retention, no "are you sure" flow.

We do not buy lists, we do not pre-tick anything, and subscribing is never bundled with another feature. If you never touch the form, we never hold your address.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. We also write nothing to your browser's local storage or session storage. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

You can confirm this: open this page, open your browser's developer tools, and look at the storage and cookie panels. They are empty.

Analytics

We do not run any web analytics on this site. There is no tracking script, no pixel, no Google Analytics, and nothing from static.cloudflareinsights.com loads on our pages.

Visiting this site sets no cookies (verified 2026-09-28: the server sends no Set-Cookie header) and we keep no per-visitor measurement. If that ever changes, this section will be updated first.

Affiliate links and commissions

As of {{LAST_UPDATED}} this site uses no affiliate links. Every outbound link to a provider goes to that provider's own public page with nothing added — no referral parameter, no tracking code, and no redirect of ours in between. You can check this by hovering any provider link on this site and reading the URL.

We hold affiliate accounts with some of the providers listed here, and we may start using affiliate links later. If we do, three things will be true, and this page will say so before the first such link goes live:

The link will carry a referral parameter, so the provider can tell the visit came from here. It will not change the price you see on this site — prices are read from the provider's own public page either way, and they are the provider's prices, not ours. And a commission is paid by the provider, never by you.

A commission does not buy a place on this site. Which providers appear, and in what order, is decided by the rules described on the about page, not by who pays.

Third-party advertising

As of {{LAST_UPDATED}} this site displays no third-party advertising. There is no ad network, no ad slot, no sponsored placement, and no advertising script on any page — you can confirm this in your browser's developer tools.

We do plan to show third-party advertising on this site once our applications to ad networks are approved. When that happens, this section will be updated before the first ad renders, and it will name the networks running here. What will be true then, and is worth knowing now:

  • Ads are served by the ad network's own scripts, not by us. Those scripts may set cookies or use similar browser storage to choose and measure ads.
  • How a network uses that data is governed by the network's own privacy policy, not this page — we will link it here when the first network goes live.
  • Advertising does not change our data: which providers appear, and in what order, is decided by the rules on the about page, not by who pays.

Hosting and server logs

This is a static site served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare processes the requests that reach it and may keep standard request data (such as IP address, user agent, and the page requested) in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use that data to identify anyone.

Cloudflare's own handling of that data is described in the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Where the deal data comes from

The prices and offers shown here are read automatically from the providers' own public pricing and promotions pages — the same pages anyone can open in a browser. No personal data is involved in that, and no login, private page, or account of anyone's is ever accessed.

Your choices

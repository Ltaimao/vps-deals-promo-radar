About this site

{{BRAND_DISPLAY}} is an independent, automated price tracker for VPS hosting. It publishes current public pricing and promotions from {{PROVIDER_COUNT}} hosting providers, and rebuilds itself every 6 hours from those providers' own pages.

Who runs it

This site is run by a single independent publisher. That is the whole of it. We are not going to dress it up with a company name, a team page, or a list of credentials — because we do not have any of those, and inventing them would make everything else on this site worth less.

Because it is one person, the fastest way to reach that person is email: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. There is more on the contact page.

What this site is not

We are not a hosting provider. We do not sell hosting, take orders, process payments, or provide technical support. We have no access to any provider's systems or to any customer account. If you need help with a server you bought, the provider is the only party who can help you.

We are not owned by, operated by, or endorsed by any of the providers listed here, and no provider has any editorial control over what appears on this site. For how outbound links work, see the privacy policy.

How a provider gets listed

A provider is included when three things hold: it runs its own hosting service, it publishes a public pricing or promotions page, and that page answers with HTTP 200 when we fetch it. Nothing else is considered.

There is no way to pay to be added, to be moved up the list, or to stay on it. Providers we cannot read are marked as unreachable rather than quietly removed or filled in from somewhere else.

How the numbers are produced

Every price on this site is read from the provider's own public page by an automated script. Nothing is estimated, converted, or invented. When a page cannot be read, the offer is shown without a price rather than with a guess.

Currency is reported exactly as the provider publishes it. We apply no exchange-rate conversion, so a figure here is the figure the provider wrote.

Where a provider's page contains more qualifying offers than we extract, the count is written as 12+ rather than a flat number: that means "at least this many", and we do not claim to know the exact total. A count without a plus sign is exact.

Corrections

If something here is wrong — a price, a link, a provider whose pages have changed — email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and it will be looked at. Fixes are made in the source and the site is rebuilt from it, so a correction holds for every future refresh too.

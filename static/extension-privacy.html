Privacy Policy — VPS Deal Alert

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What this extension does

VPS Deal Alert shows VPS price comparisons while you browse VPS provider websites. It displays a small badge with the lowest tracked price for the provider you are visiting, and a popup with the cheapest current deals.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically, we do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Browsing history or URLs you visit
  • Location data
  • Any identifiers that could be linked to you

Data the extension handles

  • Public price data: The extension fetches a public deals snapshot from https://www.vpsdealswire.com/api/deals-snapshot no more than once every 6 hours. This contains only VPS provider names and prices — no user data.
  • Local cache: The fetched price data is cached in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) to reduce server requests. This cache never leaves your device.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any user data in the first place.

Permissions explained

  • storage: Used only to cache public price data locally for 6 hours.
  • Host access to VPS provider sites: Used only to display the price-comparison badge on those sites. The extension does not read, record, or transmit anything from the pages you visit.
  • https://www.vpsdealswire.com/*: Used only to fetch the public deals snapshot.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the extension will be updated accordingly.

How Much Does a VPS Cost Per Month? Real Numbers From 225 Live Prices

Every number on this page comes from this site's own price tracker — 225 live offers across 27 providers, snapshot taken 2026-10-03. Hosting roundups love to answer "how much does a VPS cost" with a single number. The honest answer needs three numbers, because "the average" depends on which question you are really asking.

Fifteen of the 24 providers have a real entry plan at $5/month or less, and 22 of 24 sit at $10/month or less. So when HostingAdvice says you will pay "around $5 to $30 per month" for a VPS, that range is real — but most of the market's entry points cluster at the bottom of it. The rest of this guide explains how we computed these figures, why other sites quote different numbers, and what pushes your own bill up or down.

Why three "averages" can all be true at once

The median entry price ($4.70) is the cheapest real plan at each provider, sorted, with the middle one taken. It answers the shopper's question: "what is the smallest serious monthly commitment I can make?"

The median plan price (about $17) looks at every plan row in the snapshot, not just the cheapest at each provider. It answers: "if I pick a plan without hunting for the cheapest, what is typical?" This is the figure that drags "averages" upward — it includes mid-tier and managed rows, not just the headline deals.

Gizmodo's 2026 testing put self-managed 4 GB plans at "about $4 to $7 a month" on promotional terms and 8 GB plans at "about $8 to $14" — a per-size average. Ours is a per-listing average. Both are honest; they slice the same market differently. The number that matters is the one that matches the question you are asking.

What actually moves your number up or down

Renewal pricing. The entry price is the price of month one. In our 2026-10-03 snapshot, Hostinger's rows include $6.49 and $11.99 — the latter's scraped listing is titled "Renews at", which is the renewal signal we described in our renewals and hidden fees guide. Always price the second year, not the first month.

Managed vs unmanaged. Every figure above is self-managed: you get root access and a support ticket queue. Gizmodo's 2026 figures put managed 4 GB plans at "about $20 a month" and up — several times the self-managed equivalent. If you need someone else to patch the server, budget for a different market.

Promo terms. A $5/month sticker that requires 24 months paid upfront is a $120 commitment, not a $5 decision. Our tracker records the listed price as scraped; it cannot see the checkout page's fine print, so treat every headline price as the start of the question, not the answer.

Hourly noise. $0.01 rows in raw scrapes are per-hour fragments, not $0.01/month plans. They were filtered out of every median on this page. Unfiltered, they would drag the "average" down to nonsense.

How to use these numbers when you shop

Pick a band, not a number. $5/month puts you on the entry shelf at 15 providers — enough for a VPN, a static site, or a staging box. $10/month covers 22 of 24 providers' entry plans and buys breathing room. $17/month is the median plan: if a quote lands well above it for the same size box, ask what the extra money buys.

Then run the three checks our data cannot run for you: the renewal price, the billing term behind the sticker, and whether the plan is managed or self-managed. A $4.70 median entry price is good news — it just is not the whole story.
